# Wanderbots

A puzzle game about guiding a stream of little robots that walk blindly forward
through hazardous caves and into a warp gate. You give individual bots jobs to
reshape the terrain and steer the crowd.

## How to play on a PC

No install needed. Download or clone this repo and **double-click `index.html`**.
It opens in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).

## Controls

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Pick a skill | Click its button or press `1`–`7` |
| Give a bot that skill | Click the bot |
| Pause (you can still hand out skills) | `Space` / `P` |
| Fast-forward ×3 | `F` |
| Change drop rate | `+` / `-` |
| Restart level | `R` |
| Menu | `Esc` |

## Skills

- **Climb** (permanent): scales vertical walls.
- **Glide** (permanent): opens a canopy so long falls are safe.
- **Block**: stands firm and turns other bots around.
- **Spring**: becomes a springboard that flings walkers over gaps and up ledges.
- **Build**: lays a rising bridge of 12 planks.
- **Tunnel**: bores sideways through earth.
- **Dig**: digs straight down.

Steel plates can't be dug or tunnelled. Bots survive falls of about 90 px, and
acid is always fatal. A blocker or spring can be put back to work by giving it
Build, Tunnel or Dig.

## Levels

1. **Dig In**: dig through a slab, avoiding the steel plate.
2. **Mind the Gap**: chain builders across an acid chasm.
3. **Over the Wall**: climb a tower and glide down the far side.
4. **Tunnel Vision**: tunnel through hills and build over a steel step.
5. **Spring Loaded**: launch the crowd over acid pits and up a ledge.
6. **Grand Descent**: a three-tier level that uses most of the toolkit.

Your progress is saved in the browser.
