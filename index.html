WANDERBOTS

A crate of tiny maintenance robots has been dumped into the caves. They walk wherever their feet take them, straight into acid if nobody stops them.
Hand out jobs to steer enough of them into the warp gate.

Click a skill, then click a bot to give it that job.
1–7 pick a skill   Space/P pause
F fast-forward   R restart   Esc menu
+ / − change how fast bots drop in
You can hand out skills while paused.
Bots survive falls of about 90 px. Acid is always fatal.
Blocked or sprung bots can be given Build, Tunnel or Dig to put them back to work.
Steel (grey plates) can't be dug or tunnelled.
